FAQ & support

Good questions, straight answers.

The important details about migration, privacy, recovery, and what this developer preview does today.

Which cloud contact providers can I migrate from?

You can import from supported CardDAV and Exchange accounts connected through Apple Contacts, including iCloud. Enable Contacts for the account in Apple’s system settings, then select it in Proto Contacts’ Accounts settings. See the import guide.

Does importing erase my Apple Contacts originals?

Cloud-account originals are kept by default. For an eligible imported account, enable Settings → Apple Contacts Integration → Accounts → Remove Validated Originals to allow removal after validation. Back up and check validation first.

Why does Apple Contacts show more cards than the vault?

Proto Contacts can combine several matching cards into one vault contact. Apple Contacts may still contain the original cards, shared copies, and contacts you haven’t imported.

With editable local sharing on iPhone/iPad, System Contacts Sharing → Additional Apple Contacts records shows linked extra local copies and cleanup status. See local duplicate cleanup for details.

What happens when I delete a contact?

In Proto Contacts: deleting a vault contact withdraws its shared copy. With editable sharing on iPhone/iPad, it can also remove a linked, unchanged local original; changed data needs review and Me Card protection still applies.

In Apple Contacts: deleting a card leaves its imported vault contact in place until you review it. See the deletion and restoration guide for your options.

Is my Me Card backed up when Include Me Card is off?

Yes, when its account is selected for import. Turning Include Me Card off keeps the card in the encrypted vault, protects its source originals, and withholds this device’s shared copy.

Can I leave setup and finish later?

Yes. Use Continue Setup in Settings or the ellipsis menu to resume saved progress. Automatic original removal stays paused until setup is complete.

Why move out of ordinary iCloud Contacts?

Apple does not end-to-end encrypt iCloud Contacts, even with Advanced Data Protection. Proto Contacts encrypts vault contents before cloud sync, providing zero-access storage for that content. Remaining native account copies and fields shared with system Contacts are readable outside the vault.

Why move out of other cloud Contacts?

Ordinary CardDAV sync gives the account server readable contacts. Encrypting the connection does not provide zero-access encryption. Proto Contacts encrypts vault contents before upload and decrypts them on your devices. Originals left in cloud accounts and fields shared with system Contacts remain readable outside the vault.

Does the app send my contacts to you?

No. The app does not send us your address book, and we run no analytics, advertising, or sync server. See the privacy policy for storage and sharing details.

How do I sync another device?

Keep Encrypted Vault Sync enabled on each device and use the same Apple Account with Passwords & Keychain enabled. The app retrieves the key and encrypted records automatically. Sync Now requests an update.

What if devices created separate vaults?

Local contacts are kept when devices join the shared vault. Matching cards can combine; uncertain matches and conflicting values need review. In contact details, Merge history → Undo merge separates a merged card while keeping later edits on the survivor.

Will every field migrate?

Some fields are unavailable through Apple’s public Contacts API, including Contact Poster designs. For supported fields, Import & Review → Validation Details identifies incomplete imports. Keep originals or backups for anything the app cannot preserve.

Can I share only some contacts or fields?

Yes. In System Contacts Sharing, leave Share All on to share all except the Mask List, or turn it off to share only the Share List. Use Choose published fields to select fields; all supported fields start selected. These settings control Proto Contacts’ shared copies, not independent Apple Contacts cards or data already saved by other apps.

Does system sharing include Contact Posters?

Apple’s public Contacts API exposes contact photos, but not Contact Poster designs. Selected photos can be shared; the full-screen Posters cannot be recreated in Proto Contacts’ shared or exported cards. Keep original Apple Contacts cards if you need their Posters. See the Poster limitation.

Where do shared contacts appear?

iPhone/iPad: shared cards appear in read-only Contact Provider while any cloud Contacts account is enabled. With Contacts sync off for all cloud accounts, the app can use the editable on-device iPhone or iPad address book.

Mac: On My Mac, when available. See the sharing setup guide for permissions and account controls.

Why is system sharing empty or paused?

Check your selected contacts and fields, Contacts access, destination availability, and error details in System Contacts Sharing. If On My Mac is missing, follow the Mac sharing setup. For stale or missing shared copies, use Advanced → Rebuild Shared Contacts.

Can I make a backup without relying on iCloud?

Yes. Use Encrypted Vault Sync → Advanced Recovery → Export Backup… to save a full-vault archive. Save its password and backup recovery key when both encryption layers are enabled; these are separate from the vault recovery key. Follow the backup guide and test recovery.

Can I move contacts back to iCloud or another account?

Yes. Choose Accounts → Export Contacts… and a supported destination. Preparing the export pauses automatic import and original removal on this device. Your vault is kept. The app verifies local copies; check delivery to other devices separately. Follow the export guide for steps and field limits.

What do the reset buttons do?

Reset App Settings to Defaults… restores preferences, including sharing choices, after confirmation. It keeps your vault and setup progress, leaves original removal off, and preserves any shared export pause.

Reset Database on All Devices… erases vault data and history here and on updated linked devices when they next sync, after two confirmations. Keys, preferences, and existing writable Apple Contacts cards remain; the read-only provider view is cleared. Make and verify a backup first.

Do preferences survive reinstalling?

They can. Updating, replacing, or offloading an installation can preserve local app data. Full iOS app deletion removes its local container; deleting the Mac app bundle may leave app data behind. Use Reset App Settings to Defaults… in Settings for a reliable preference reset.

What happens if I delete the app or lose a key?

Deleting the app does not restore removed Apple Contacts originals. Encrypted cloud data and independently saved copies may remain.

Losing a key: a synced vault needs its key from iCloud Keychain or your saved vault recovery key. Archives use their own password and backup recovery key. Without a required secret, that recovery route is unavailable. See the recovery guide.

Will Apple Legacy Contact unlock my vault?

Not by itself. Apple’s Legacy Contact excludes iCloud Keychain and does not provide Proto Contacts’ vault key. Plan a separate, tested recovery route for estate access. See recovery and estate access.

Is this a Proton app?

No. Proto Contacts is an independent project, with no Proton account connection or endorsement. See the Proton proposal.
