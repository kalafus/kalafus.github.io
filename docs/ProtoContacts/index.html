How it works

One private record. A deliberate way out.

The vault is the home for your contact record. Proto Contacts publishes only the contacts and fields you choose. Existing Apple Contacts cards remain separately readable until removed.

01 / BRING THEM IN

Migrate with review

Import from supported cloud accounts connected to Apple Contacts, local contacts, or a vCard. Changes and conflicts can be reviewed in the app instead of silently flattening different records.

02 / KEEP THEM PRIVATE

Encrypt the full record

Contacts, photos, notes, groups, and decisions live in the app-owned vault. Sync starts on for new vaults; zero-access encryption means the cloud provider cannot read the stored vault contents.

03 / USE THEM EVERYWHERE

Share a chosen view

Share all contacts except a Mask List, or share none except a Share List. Choose fields separately. Publish your chosen view to Apple Contacts for use by Phone and other apps.

Know what lives where

Private means knowing the boundary.

Import can be followed by removal of validated originals. System sharing publishes your chosen contacts and fields outside the vault. Each has its own controls.

Developer preview: cleanup of synced-account originals defaults off. Editable iPhone/iPad sharing can consolidate validated local duplicates. Use the resumable setup guide to choose accounts, identify your Me Card, import, validate, and review. Offline backups and reviewed export to Apple Contacts provide independent copies.
